results conﬁrm the utility and applicability of the evolutionary model. [Keywords: village ﬁssioning, social evolution, scalar stress, Lake
Titicaca, Bolivia]

The simple social evolutionary model with which I be-
ganthisarticleaccuratelydescribesthecourseoftheEarly
andMiddleFormativesequencefromthesouthernTiticaca
Basin.IntheEarlyChiripaphase,whenthelandscapewas
relativelyunoccupied,thelargestvillagesregularlyﬁssioned
oncrossingacertainpopulationthreshold.IntheMiddle

withincipientformsofpublicceremonialism,presumably
having as one goal the mitigation of scale-related social
stressandtheresolutionofinternalconﬂicts.

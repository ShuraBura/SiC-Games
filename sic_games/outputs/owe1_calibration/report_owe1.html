FINDING — cell-size consistency: GROSS INCONSISTENCY. The home-range "gate" of landing C-static-medium at ~100 km^2 is met only by setting ell = 1.34 km/cell — roughly 7x smaller than the committed ~10 km/cell geometry. Equivalently: at the committed 10 km/cell, the model's emergent median home-range is ~5600 km^2, about 56x the ~100 km^2 forager band. Per blueprint §3.5 this gross inconsistency is reported, NOT forced.

Interpretation (for supervisor): the model's C agents occupy ~56 distinct cells over a lifetime — far more mobile (in cell units) than a forager is in home-range units. Two mutually exclusive readings, neither resolvable here (locked params + committed geometry are both out of scope to retune): (a) accept ~1.3 km cells (world only ~134x134 km ≈ 17857 km^2), which is too small for the 20–60 ethnographic bands at forager density the §3.2 geometry intends; or (b) keep ~10 km cells and accept that emergent home-range is ~56x forager scale, i.e. the model's mobility does not map to forager territoriality at this resolution. This is a genuine model-vs-ethnography scale tension surfaced by the calibration, registered as a finding. Note: home-range is legitimately joint (foraging + ψ social pull, C2 mechanism) — no foraging-only isolation attempted.

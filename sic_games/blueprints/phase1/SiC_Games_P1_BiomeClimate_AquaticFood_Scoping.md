## 1. The diagnosed gaps (with data)
**Gap A — biome ↔ climate are DECOUPLED, and the temperature field is impoverished (this IS the deferred CL-1 climate stage).** The `temperature` field is a pure **latitudinal** gradient (row 0 equator 27 °C → row N-1 pole 1 °C; `terrain.py` L665) — it is **NOT seasonal** (a single static annual mean; the only seasonality in the model is a *food*-capacity multiplier `ClimateField.season()`/`a_seas` + the R-46 biome seasonal-*amplitude*, NOT temperature), **NOT elevation-dependent** (no lapse — montane reads WARM), and **NOT biome-consistent** (biome is classified from moisture/npp/elev at L595, never temperature). Consequence (occupied-cell means, R-48 probe): **savanna 11.8 °C (coldest!), montane 15.3 °C (warmest)** — inverted vs Earth. **Required temperature field:** `T(cell, month) = T_latitude(row) − LAPSE·elevation + seas_amp(cell)·seasonal_wave(month)`, with seasonal amplitude larger at high latitude / continental interiors and driven by the SAME insolation signal that already drives the food seasonality (reconcile, don't duplicate). Then **derive biome from T + moisture (Whittaker)** so biome is an OUTCOME of climate, not decoupled from it (causal note: biome follows temperature, not the reverse). This activates the deferred **CL-1** (temperature/humidity, solar-forced) — see DEFERRED_MECHANICS.

**Gap B — the aquatic resource is DISCONNECTED from the agent economy (confirmed 2026-07-03).** The Bird-1997 shore bonus (+1491.5 kcal/hr) lives in `forage_kcal`, but agents forage from `NPPCapacityField`, which is built from `npp_gm2` ALONE (`capacity.py` L42) and NEVER reads `forage_kcal`. Water cells have `npp=0`, so there is ZERO aquatic productivity in the harvest field the agents actually use. That is why `shore%`/`river%` = 0 at occupied cells — NOT because agents sit on land near water, but because coasts/rivers offer them NO extra food, so they never cluster there (verified: the biome patch DOES contain high-`forage_kcal` shore cells; agents ignore them). **So the aquatic FOOD resource is dormant model-wide.** The stage must therefore (i) build an `aquatic_food` field AND (ii) WIRE IT INTO the capacity/harvest field so productive coasts/rivers actually attract + support denser bands — only then can aquatic-based complexity emerge. A morph gate alone is insufficient.

**Q-checks that motivated this stage (supervisor, 2026-07-03):** (Q1) shore%/river%=0 → the disconnect above, NOT geographically correct. (Q2) desert occupied-cell `wateracc` 0.55 highest = a realistic SELECTION effect (desert foragers water-tethered to oases; the desert world is dry overall, 0.2% water, mean land wateracc 0.27); desert `seasonality` 0.54 highest = defensible for CLIMATE (deserts have extreme temp/rain seasonality) but the WRONG variable for storability (no seasonal food glut) — the temp-seasonality vs food-glut-seasonality conflation. (Q3) below.

## 2. Phased build

### Phase 1 — The real temperature field: latitude + elevation + SEASON (CL-1) [LOW-MED RISK]
Build `T(cell, month)` (currently latitude-only static):
- **1a Elevation lapse:** `T_air = T_latitude − LAPSE × elev_m`, `elev_m = elev × reliefAmpM`, `LAPSE = 6.5 °C/km`. Montane → cold (15.3 → ~6 °C). Physical; touches only the `temperature` field (nothing reads it except the superseded storage temp-gate → small blast radius, confirm).
- **1c Seasonal cycle:** `T(cell,month) = T_annual(cell) + T_seas_amp(cell) × cos(2π·month/12 − φ)`, with `T_seas_amp` rising with latitude + continentality (small in tropics/maritime, large in high-latitude interiors). **Drive it from the SAME insolation/obliquity signal `ClimateField` already uses for the food season** (`a_seas`) so the two seasonalities are one physical driver, not two. This gives a real winter (the store-through-winter pressure that underwrites delayed-return complexity — Testart) AND the seasonal salmon-run window.
Expected: montane cold + seasonal; tropics warm + aseasonal; interiors strongly seasonal.

### Phase 1b — RIVER temperature is SOURCE-dependent, not local air [Q3, lit-confirmed]
**Key lit finding (2026-07-03):** a river's temperature is set by its SOURCE, not local air temp — **snowmelt/montane headwaters → COLD (<15 °C summer even in alpine); lowland/pluvial → warm (tracks air); spring/aquifer-fed → thermally STABLE (cold summer, warm winter)** (IOP 2021 snowmelt regimes; Eawag; Hudson 2023 — LITERATURE.md). And **anadromous salmon need cold water: optimum ~15.9–16.5 °C, stress >15.6 °C, lethal >21–22.5 °C** (USDA-FS salmonid behavior; Conservation Physiology 2022). **Consequence:** a salmon river can be cold even at LOW latitude if it drains montane headwaters — so the fishery signal's river temperature must be `T_river = f(upstream/headwater elevation)` (a river cell inherits the cold of its high-elevation catchment), NOT the local air temp. Proxy: route the existing flow-accumulation to carry a min-upstream-elevation (or headwater `elev`) per river cell → `T_river` from that headwater's lapse-adjusted temperature. (Full hydrological routing is the cost here — see RT-3.)

### Phase 2 — Biome ↔ climate coupling: Whittaker-consistent labels [HIGH BLAST RADIUS]
Real biomes are a function of **temperature × moisture** (Whittaker). Re-derive/relabel so a cold+dry cell is STEPPE/TUNDRA (not "savanna"), a hot+wet cell tropical FOREST, hot+seasonal SAVANNA, etc. — so "savanna" is WARM by construction. **Red-team RT-1 (blast radius):** biome keys FORAGE_KCAL_TARGETS, GAME_KCAL_TARGETS, GAME_MOBILITY, BIOME_SEASONAL_AMP, capacity, and every prior biome→society result (R-37/R-45/R-46/R-48). Re-deriving biomes re-numbers the whole world → ALL those must be re-validated. **Mitigation options:** (a) FULL re-derivation (most correct, big re-validation); (b) LIGHTER — keep biome placement, add a temperature-consistency RELABEL only for cold-misplaced cells (cold "savanna"→steppe) so labels/seasonality are climate-consistent without re-numbering productivity; (c) DEFER Phase 2 — use the PHYSICAL aquatic-food field (Phase 3) for the morph, independent of biome labels, and accept the label imperfection as a reporting caveat. **Recommend (c)/(b) first; (a) only if the biome map itself must be realistic.**

### Phase 3 — Aquatic-food fishery field, WIRED INTO the harvest field [THE NEW SIGNAL]
A per-cell `aquatic_food` ∈ [0,1] = dense-storable-aquatic-food density, from physical fields (independent of biome label):
- **Anadromous (salmon):** `coldness(T_river) × river-access × sea-connectivity × productivity`. `coldness = clamp((T_lethal − T_river)/(T_lethal − T_opt), 0, 1)` with `T_opt≈16, T_lethal≈21` (lit); **`T_river` = SOURCE temperature (Phase 1b, headwater elevation)**, NOT local air; access from `isRiver`/`wateracc`; `sea-connectivity` from `dist`/river-reaches-water (anadromous must reach the sea).
- **Shellfish/marine:** `is_shore × npp` (coastal littoral, the Bird-bonus zone) — warm-tolerant, coastal (covers subtropical estuarine complex foragers, e.g. Calusa — RT-4).
- `aquatic_food = max(anadromous, shellfish)`. **Wire it into the harvest field** (Gap B): add an aquatic-productivity term to `NPPCapacityField` (or a parallel field summed into `level()`), calibrated so a rich fishery cell supports a denser band (the storage→complexity substrate). Lit anchors: Ames 1994 / Testart 1982 (NW-Coast storage complexity), Binford 2001 (aquatic-dependence ~ ET), salmon-thermal (USDA-FS / Conservation Physiology 2022), river-thermal-by-source (IOP 2021 / Eawag / Hudson 2023). Constants PROVISIONAL → sweep + sign-off.

### Phase 4 — Morph gate on aquatic_food [REPLACES the R-48 heuristic]
Complexity requires `mean(aquatic_food over band cells) ≥ threshold` (single clean signal), replacing the `wateracc×seasonality × npp_floor` heuristic (R-48). Expected: cold productive salmon rivers/coasts → complex (montane w/ Phase 1, temperate coasts); warm tropical (Mbuti forest, Hadza savanna once warm) → egalitarian; desert → egalitarian. `stratified` still needs settlement density (separate).

## 4. Red-team (beyond RT-1)
- **RT-2 — does elevation lapse make montane survive?** Colder montane may raise mortality/change capacity if anything reads T. Verify montane still sustains a society.
- **RT-3 — sea-connectivity is hard on a torus.** Anadromous needs "river reaches sea." Proxy by `dist`-to-water + `is_shore` proximity rather than true hydrological routing (a full routing is out of scope).
- **RT-4 — warm-water fisheries exist** (tropical reefs, Calusa estuaries — subtropical). The shellfish term (is_shore × npp, temperature-agnostic) covers these so complexity isn't *only* cold. Keep both terms.
- **RT-5 — calibration explosion.** Many new constants (lapse, salmon T-cutoff, term weights, threshold). Ship ablatable/default-OFF; lock only after a sweep + sign-off; keep the R-48 heuristic as the fallback until the fishery field is validated.
- **RT-6 — over-reach.** This is a genuine substrate stage (climate + a new resource field), not a tweak. Phase 1 is cheap and high-value (fixes montane); Phases 2–4 are the real project. Land Phase 1 first, re-assess.

## 5. Recommendation (build order)
1. **Phase 1a elevation lapse** — cheap, physical; alone flips montane cold (salmon side). Land + verify montane survives (RT-2).
2. **Phase 1c seasonal temperature** — the real winter (reconciled with the existing food-season insolation driver). Med risk (new dynamics: winter mortality/storage pressure) → validate.
3. **Gap-B: wire aquatic food into the capacity field** — prerequisite for ANY aquatic complexity (currently dormant); test that agents begin clustering on productive coasts/rivers.
4. **Phase 3+4 aquatic-food fishery field + morph gate** (uses river-source T from 1a/1b) — the storable-aquatic complexity driver, superseding the R-48 heuristic.
5. **Phase 2 (Whittaker biome-from-T+moisture)** — do LAST or defer; highest blast radius. With a real seasonal temperature field (1a/1c) the biome derivation becomes well-posed, but re-numbering biomes re-validates everything downstream.

# SiC Games — Phase 1 — Economic Defensibility / Owned-Patch (the *between-band* driver of concentration)

**Status:** SCOPED + RED-TEAMED 2026-07-04 — **NOT YET BUILT** (needs supervisor sign-off + the discussion items in §7 resolved before build). The missing sociological driver diagnosed after the R-51 saturation long-run: on a normal patch the population equilibrates at ~7% of the food ceiling, band footprint-density stays flat at ~0.015/km² (6× below Binford packing 0.091), 0% packed — food is superabundant relative to a demographically-limited population, so **there is no scarcity pressure to concentrate**, and IFD's *free access* shares every rich cell down to the landscape average.
**The reframe (why this, not another circumscription patch):** the famous complex foragers (NW Coast, Calusa, Chumash, Jōmon, Natufian) did **not** emerge from scarcity — they emerged in resource-**rich** settings by **owning and defending** concentrated, storable, predictable resources. The dominant empirical path to sedentism is *ownership of abundance*, not *squeezing by scarcity*. Our model reproduces the mobile-egalitarian **default** correctly (~95% of forager societies); it cannot yet express the **exception** because movement is Ideal *Free* Distribution — nobody can monopolize a salmon reach, so it dilutes to average and never becomes a defensible surplus. This blueprint adds the exclusion that converts *free access → owned access*.
**Anchors:** Dyson-Hudson & Smith 1978 (*Human Territoriality: An Ecological Reassessment*, Am. Anthropol. 80:21–41 — the **economic-defensibility model**: territoriality pays iff a resource is **dense + predictable**; open access otherwise); Testart 1982 & Woodburn 1982 (delayed-return systems have **property rights** over stored/concentrated resources; immediate-return systems do not); Ames 1994 (NW-Coast **owned, heritable** salmon stations / ranked house-ownership); Sahlins 1972 & Kelly *Foraging Spectrum* (foragers sit **below** food carrying capacity — the limit is disease/mobility/social, not food, so scarcity is the wrong lever); Carneiro 1970 (circumscription — the *scarcity* path, kept as the complement, not the primary).

## 1. The mechanism (one coupling: *free access → conditional exclusion*)
`resource density × predictability → defensibility → claim → exclusion → owner keeps per-capita high → real storable surplus → tether (StorageSedentism) → concentration → packing → morph`.

The single new idea: a cell can be **owned** by a band, and an *outsider* evaluating that cell in `diffusion_select_target` perceives a **reduced** expected share (the shadow of defense), so IFD routes outsiders elsewhere and the **owner band keeps the cell's yield split only among its own members** — per-capita stays high, the glut is bankable, tethering finally pays. Ownership is **conditional on economic defensibility**, so it fires only where anthropology says it should (dense predictable gluts — the aquatic cells), leaving 90%+ of the diffuse terrestrial landscape as open-access IFD (unchanged).

This is the *between-band* complement to `…_StorageSedentism_Scoping.md`: **defensibility makes the surplus private (this doc); sedentism decides to stay and bank it (that doc).** Sedentism's `C-PAYOFF` gate is negative without this — under free access a stored granary on a shared cell is just diluted, so the two must be reconciled (§6, RT-3).

## 2. Defensibility index — *what* is ownable (the Dyson-Hudson & Smith gate)
A cell is claimable only when defending it pays. Economic-defensibility index, from EFC fields (resource-agnostic — reads `S_pot`/`aquatic_food` now, `cultivability` later):
- **Density** `ρ(cell)` — the concentrated harvestable value per unit area (high on salmon reaches / rich coasts; low on diffuse forest/savanna NPP).
- **Predictability** `π(cell)` — inverse temporal/spatial uncertainty (a salmon run returns to the *same reach* every year → high π; a dispersed unpredictable game field → low π). See §7-Q4 (metric).
- `D(cell) = ρ · π`. **Claimable iff `D ≥ D_min`.** Anchor: DH&S — defense is worthwhile only in the dense-and-predictable quadrant; sparse or unpredictable resources stay open-access (defense cost > benefit). This *auto-selects the aquatic cells* and excludes the diffuse interior, matching the GATE-3 observation that exactly those rich aquatic cells fail to concentrate today.

## 3. Ownership + exclusion (the movement seam)
- **Claim state:** a band (`band_id`, reuse GroupVector) that *occupies a claimable cell for ≥ `claim_dwell` steps* claims it → `_cell_owner[(x,y)] = band_id`. Heritable/persistent with hysteresis; released when the owner band abandons it for `claim_lapse` steps or the resource fails (`D` drops).
- **Exclusion in `diffusion_select_target`:** when a candidate cell is owned by a band the agent does **not** belong to, its perceived per-capita yield is multiplied by `exclusion_factor ∈ [0,1)` (soft "shadow of conflict" — the intruder anticipates a defended, low-yield encounter and IFD routes it away). Owners perceive the full share. This is a *perception/utility* change only — no explicit violence in v1 (§7-Q2).
- **Owner retains the split:** on an owned cell, the harvest split (`compute_harvest_shares`) runs over **members only** (outsiders were routed away, or contribute weight 0), so the owner band's per-capita on its salmon reach stays high instead of diluting to landscape average — *this is the whole point*.
- **Defense cost:** the owner pays a small maintenance cost scaled by **intrusion pressure** (how many outsiders tried to enter) — so on an empty landscape defense is free (no intruders) and on a crowded one it is costly but worth it (dense resource). DH&S cost/benefit is thus **emergent**, not hand-set.

## 4. Implementation seams
- `defensibility_index(fields)` helper → `D` field; `D_min` gate. (Density from `aquatic_food`/`S_pot`; predictability metric per §7-Q4.)
- `TerrainWorld._cell_owner: dict[(x,y)->band_id]` + `_claim_dwell` counters, maintained in `_maintain_bands`/`_step_rivalrous` alongside the existing per-band bookkeeping.
- `diffusion_select_target(...)`: add an `owner_of(cell)` + `agent_band` check → apply `exclusion_factor` to a non-owner's perceived per-capita. **`enable_economic_defensibility=False` ⇒ the check is skipped ⇒ bit-exact.**
- Harvest: on owned cells, restrict the occupant list / zero outsider weight in the split.
- Morph unchanged — it already reads density+surplus; exclusion just lets the density/surplus actually accumulate on the owned cells.

## 5. Conditions a band must meet to *own + realise* (mirrors StorageSedentism §2, from the defense side)
- **D-DEFENSIBLE** — the cell's `D ≥ D_min` (dense + predictable). *(DH&S gate.)*
- **D-PRESENCE** — the band actually occupies it for `claim_dwell` (you defend what you hold).
- **D-LABOUR** — enough members to defend + work it (shared with the sedentism labour gate; small bands can't hold a contested reach). *Ames NW-Coast labour.*
- **D-NET-BENEFIT** — kept-surplus (undiluted per-capita × storability) minus defense cost > the IFD value of moving. *(This is the myopia fix from the exclusion side; ties to sedentism `C-PAYOFF`.)*
- **Release** when `D` fails, the band leaves, or net benefit goes negative → open access resumes (the boom→bust seam for the dynastic cycle).

## 6. Red-team
- **RT-1 [landscape lock-up / starvation].** Over-claiming fences the map → newcomers can't forage → extinction. *Mitigation:* only `D ≥ D_min` cells (rare aquatic, 1–6%) are claimable; 90%+ diffuse interior stays open IFD. Exclusion is *soft* (perceived-yield penalty), never a hard wall in v1 — a starving intruder can still take a diluted share. Validate interior egalitarian preserved + no extinction (GATE-3 re-run).
- **RT-2 [becomes a warfare model].** Explicit violence/mortality on intrusion → death spiral + scope creep. *Mitigation:* v1 is **shadow-of-conflict only** (utility penalty, no deaths). Actual raiding/Carneiro-warfare is a *later* explicit layer (§7-Q2), off by default.
- **RT-3 [conflict/overlap with StorageSedentism + C4 footprint].** Three movement forces now act: C4 footprint *spreads* families (marginal biomes), sedentism *tightens* onto the fishery, defensibility *excludes outsiders* from the fishery. *Mitigation:* they act in different regimes and compose — defensibility only on `D≥D_min` cells (rich aquatic), C4 dispersal elsewhere; defensibility is the *prerequisite* that makes sedentism's `C-PAYOFF` positive. **Must be built/tested jointly with StorageSedentism, not independently** (§7-Q3).
- **RT-4 [predictability metric is thin].** `aquatic_food`/NPP are annual; we have `season()`/seasonal amplitude but not true inter-annual variance. *Mitigation:* v1 proxy π by **resource type/seasonal-return signature** (aquatic anadromous = high π by construction; diffuse NPP = low π); flag as PROVISIONAL, refine when a variance field exists (§7-Q4).
- **RT-5 [IFD still pulls owners off the patch].** Even owners maximise per-capita; once members fill the owned cell to member-average they may spread. *Mitigation:* exclusion keeps *member* per-capita high (outsiders gone) so the incentive to stay is real — but the *tether* (StorageSedentism sedentism_factor) is what actually holds them; exclusion alone concentrates *less*. Measure both; expect defensibility+sedentism together to cross packing, defensibility alone to only raise density partway.
- **RT-6 [determinism / bit-exactness].** New per-cell owner lookups + a members-only split must not perturb the legacy path. *Mitigation:* single `enable_economic_defensibility` flag; when OFF, `owner_of ≡ None`, split + movement untouched → assert bit-exact on the R-18/19/E.3 suite.
- **RT-7 [calibration + circular validation].** New knobs (`D_min`, `exclusion_factor`, `claim_dwell/lapse`, defense cost). *Mitigation:* ship ablatable/default-OFF; sweep; **validate by mechanism** — do bands claim + concentrate on cold salmon reaches / rich coasts *without* tuning to a target stratification rate? — not by curve-fitting.
- **RT-8 [emergent commons/stewardship interaction with GD-1].** With exclusion, an owner forages *only* its patch → GD-1 depletion concentrates there; without exclusion the commons is over-grazed equally. Could *help* (owner conserves, Ostrom) or *hurt* (owner over-depletes its own reach). *Mitigation:* observe; do not hand-wire stewardship (§7-Q5).
- **RT-9 [generality].** No aquatic-specific logic in the defensibility/exclusion path — it must read `S_pot`/`D` so proto-ag `cultivability` drops in for free. Enforce in review (shared with StorageSedentism RT-7).

## 7. Open questions — **need discussion to resolve before build**
- **Q1 — Ownership grain.** Band (`band_id`, ~25 people) vs family vs lineage? *Recommend band-level* (matches GroupVector + NW-Coast house/lineage ownership ≈ our band scale). Family-level risks fragmenting a reach; lineage needs machinery we don't have.
- **Q2 — Exclusion mechanism.** Soft "shadow of conflict" (perceived-yield penalty, no deaths) vs hard bar vs explicit conflict-with-mortality? *Recommend soft v1*; hard/violent as a later gated layer. (Determines whether this stays a settlement model or becomes a warfare model.)
- **Q3 — Relationship to StorageSedentism.** Build defensibility **first** as the prerequisite, **together** as one settlement mechanism, or sedentism-then-defensibility? *Recommend building them together* (defensibility makes the private surplus; sedentism tethers to it — neither concentrates alone). This affects sequencing + the RESULTS story.
- **Q4 — Predictability metric π.** Resource-type proxy (aquatic=predictable) vs seasonal-return signature vs a real inter-annual variance field (new work)? *Recommend the type/seasonal proxy for v1*, flagged PROVISIONAL.
- **Q5 — GD-1 stewardship coupling.** Let ownership *emergently* change depletion (owner forages only its patch), or explicitly model conservation (Ostrom common-pool management)? *Recommend emergent-only in v1*, observe, decide later.
- **Q6 — Does exclusion alone suffice, or is the scarcity/circumscription lever still needed?** Hypothesis: exclusion unlocks the *rich-abundance* path (NW-Coast) without scarcity; circumscription remains the *separate* path for agricultural packing. *Recommend treating them as two independent, co-existing routes* (matches the two-channel finding from the saturation run) — validate each fires in its own setting.

## 8. Validation (once resolved + built)
- Aquatic worlds: bands **claim** cold salmon reaches / rich coasts → outsiders routed away → owner per-capita stays high → footprint tightens (with sedentism) → density crosses Binford 0.091 → morph → complex/stratified; interior diffuse cells stay open-access mobile-egalitarian. GATE-3 re-run: **does exclusion finally produce real spatial concentration on the aquatic cells** (the thing C8's subsidy could not)?
- Ablation: defensibility-only vs sedentism-only vs both — expect *both* required to cross packing (RT-5).
- Generality smoke-test: swap `S_pot`/`D` to a synthetic `cultivability` field → same claiming/concentration (proves resource-agnosticism).
- Regression: default-OFF ⇒ bit-exact; R-18/19/E.3 mobile-forager equilibria unchanged.

## 9. Recommendation
This is the higher-leverage next mechanism over another circumscription patch: it is the *diagnosed* missing driver (free access → no private surplus → no tether), it is the empirically dominant path to forager complexity (owned abundance, DH&S/Testart/Ames), and it directly targets the unresolved GATE-3 failure. Build it **jointly with StorageSedentism**, `enable_economic_defensibility` default-OFF, soft-exclusion v1, resource-agnostic — **after** the §7 discussion items (esp. Q2 exclusion mechanism and Q3 sequencing) are resolved with the supervisor.
